Rocket Raccoon|His gun is much more effective at short range than at medium or long range, so shooting from a distance lowers both his accuracy and his damage.
Rocket Raccoon|Many Rocket players stand farther from their team than other Strategists because his healing orb reaches a long way. Low accuracy and low damage on Rocket usually reflect that distance, not poor aim, so coach it as a positioning trade-off (when it's safe to move closer and add damage) rather than as an aim problem.
